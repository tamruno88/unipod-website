Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026 Last Updated: July 27, 2026

At Unipod, we respect your privacy. We follow a data minimization approach, collecting only the information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve your learning experience. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at support@runocreative.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, and sign-in credentials through services like Google or Sign in with Apple. This information is used to authenticate your account and sync your data across devices.

Learning Data

To provide your learning experience, we store information such as your selected languages, followed podcasts, listening progress, saved episodes, downloaded content, flashcards, saved vocabulary, and other learning preferences.

Device Data

Certain information is stored on your device, such as cached transcripts, downloaded content, playback preferences, and other settings that improve app performance and offline access.

Technical Information

We collect technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve Unipod, such as authentication tokens, app version, device information, security-related data, and diagnostic information. We may also collect usage and analytics information, such as feature interactions, app performance, and engagement metrics, to help us understand how people use Unipod and improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and operate Unipod
  • Sync your account and learning progress across your devices
  • Personalize your learning experience
  • Analyze app usage and engagement to understand how users interact with Unipod, improve existing features, develop new functionality, troubleshoot issues, and enhance the overall user experience
  • Protect the security and integrity of Unipod and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Communicate with you about your account, important service updates, and, where permitted by law, product news and feature announcements

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your personal information only when necessary to provide and operate Unipod, such as with trusted service providers that help us authenticate users, process payments, provide cloud infrastructure, support AI-powered features, analyze app performance, and protect the security of our services.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

4. International Data Transfers

Your personal information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate. We take reasonable measures to protect your information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide Unipod and comply with legal obligations.

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, learning progress, followed podcasts, saved episodes, flashcards, vocabulary, and other personal data associated with your account.

6. Your Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, update, export, or delete your personal information, as well as object to or restrict certain processing. Where we rely on your consent to process your personal information, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@runocreative.com.

If you live in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with applicable privacy laws, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

Unipod is not intended for children under the age of 13, or the minimum digital age required by the laws of your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you within the app or by other appropriate means. The updated version will always include the latest effective date.
